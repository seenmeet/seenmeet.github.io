Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026 · Last updated: October 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to all mobile apps and games published by DouZhuan Studio ("we", "us") on Google Play, the Apple App Store and other platforms (each an "App"). It explains what information is collected when you use our Apps, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Not every App includes every feature or third-party service described below. Each section applies only to the Apps that use the feature it describes.

1. Information we collect

Our Apps do not require you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or other contact details. We do not operate servers that store your personal information.

1.1 Data stored on your device

Our Apps save data locally on your device so they work as expected, for example game progress, saved games, settings and preferences, and purchase status. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the App.

1.2 Photos and files you choose

Some Apps let you pick a photo or file, for example to use your own picture in a game. The App uses the system picker, so it only receives the items you select, not your whole library. Selected content is processed and stored locally on your device. We do not upload it to our servers or share it with third parties.

1.3 Information collected automatically by third-party services

When an App shows ads or processes purchases, the third-party services described in Section 3 may automatically collect information such as:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Google Advertising ID (Android) or IDFA (iOS, only if you allow tracking);
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system, language, screen size, network type and app version;
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • Usage and interaction data, such as ads viewed or clicked;
  • Diagnostic data, such as crash logs and performance data.

1.4 Content requests

Some Apps download content, such as images or levels, from our content servers or content delivery networks. These requests include your IP address and basic technical information, which are used only to deliver the content.

2. How information is used

  • To provide the App's features and save your progress;
  • To show ads, limit how often ads appear, measure ad performance and prevent fraud;
  • To process purchases and restore them;
  • To fix problems and improve our Apps.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Third-party services

Our Apps may use the following third-party services. They collect and process information under their own privacy policies:

  • AppLovin (MAX) – advertising and ad mediation: https://www.applovin.com/privacy/. AppLovin may work with other ad networks (mediation partners), which are listed in its policy.
  • Google AdMob – advertising: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Play services / Google Play Billing – app distribution and in-app purchases: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Apple App Store / StoreKit – app distribution and in-app purchases: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

4. Advertising and your choices

Free versions of our Apps may display ads. Ads may be personalized based on the information described in Section 1.3. You can control this:

  • iOS: the App asks for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (usually Settings › Privacy › Ads).
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: the App asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. Where provided, you can change your choice from the privacy option inside the App.
  • Remove ads: where offered, a paid upgrade (such as VIP or "Remove Ads") turns off ads.

5. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple or Google. Your payment information is handled by them and is never shared with us. The App only receives purchase confirmation information, such as the product ID and transaction status, so it can unlock content and restore purchases.

6. Sharing and external links

If you share content from an App, it is passed to your device's system share function, and you choose where it goes. Our Apps may contain links to external websites, such as credits for images. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites.

7. Children's privacy

Our Apps are intended for a general audience and are not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law requires). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through our Apps, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

8. Data retention and security

Data stored on your device remains until you delete it or uninstall the App. Data collected by third-party services is retained according to their policies. We take reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, object to or restrict its processing, and withdraw consent at any time. Because we do not store your personal information on our servers, you can exercise most of these rights directly on your device: delete data in the App, uninstall the App, change your consent choice, or reset your advertising identifier. For data held by third-party services, you may contact them directly, or contact us and we will help where we can. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Please review it periodically.

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 7 日 · 最近更新：2026 年 10 月 7 日

本隐私政策适用于 DouZhuan Studio（下称"我们"）在 Google Play、Apple App Store 及其他平台发布的所有移动应用和游戏（下称"应用"），说明你使用我们的应用时会收集哪些信息、如何使用，以及你拥有的选择。

并非每款应用都包含下文所述的全部功能或第三方服务。各条款仅适用于使用了相应功能的应用。

1. 我们收集的信息

我们的应用无需注册账号，不会索取你的姓名、邮箱、手机号等联系方式。我们不运营存储你个人信息的服务器。

1.1 保存在你设备上的数据

为了正常运行，应用会在你的设备本地保存数据，例如游戏进度、存档、设置偏好和购买状态。这些数据只保留在你的设备上，不会发送给我们，卸载应用时会被删除。

1.2 你选择的照片和文件

部分应用允许你选择照片或文件，例如用你自己的图片进行游戏。应用使用系统选择器，只会收到你选中的内容，无法访问你的整个相册。所选内容仅在你的设备本地处理和保存，我们不会将其上传到服务器，也不会提供给第三方。

1.3 第三方服务自动收集的信息

当应用展示广告或处理购买时，第 3 条所列的第三方服务可能自动收集以下信息：

  • 设备标识符，例如 Google 广告 ID（Android）或 IDFA（iOS，仅在你允许跟踪时）；
  • 设备和应用信息，例如设备型号、操作系统、语言、屏幕尺寸、网络类型和应用版本；
  • IP 地址，以及据此推断的大致位置；
  • 使用和互动数据，例如展示或点击的广告；
  • 诊断数据，例如崩溃日志和性能数据。

1.4 内容请求

部分应用会从我们的内容服务器或 CDN 下载图片、关卡等内容。这些请求包含你的 IP 地址和基本技术信息，仅用于传输内容。

2. 信息的用途

  • 提供应用功能并保存你的进度；
  • 展示广告、控制广告频率、衡量广告效果和防止欺诈；
  • 处理和恢复购买；
  • 修复问题、改进应用。

我们不出售你的个人信息。

3. 第三方服务

我们的应用可能使用以下第三方服务，它们依据各自的隐私政策收集和处理信息：

  • AppLovin（MAX）：广告及广告聚合，https://www.applovin.com/privacy/。AppLovin 可能与其他广告网络（聚合合作方）合作，合作方列表见其政策。
  • Google AdMob：广告，https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Play 服务 / Google Play 结算：应用分发和应用内购买，https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Apple App Store / StoreKit：应用分发和应用内购买，https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

4. 广告及你的选择

应用的免费版本可能展示广告，广告可能基于第 1.3 条所述信息进行个性化。你可以通过以下方式控制：

  • iOS：应用会通过 Apple 的"App 跟踪透明度"弹窗征求你的许可，你可以随时在"设置 › 隐私与安全性 › 跟踪"中更改。
  • Android：你可以在设备设置（通常为"设置 › 隐私 › 广告"）中重置或删除广告 ID，或停用个性化广告。
  • 欧洲经济区、英国和瑞士：展示个性化广告前，应用会征求你的同意。若应用内提供隐私选项，你可以在其中更改你的选择。
  • 去除广告：如应用提供付费升级（如 VIP 或"去广告"），购买后将关闭广告。

5. 应用内购买

购买由 Apple 或 Google 处理，你的支付信息由他们处理，不会提供给我们。应用只会收到购买确认信息（如商品 ID 和交易状态），用于解锁内容和恢复购买。

6. 分享和外部链接

你在应用中分享内容时，内容会交给设备系统的分享功能，由你决定分享到哪里。应用中可能包含指向外部网站的链接（例如图片署名），我们不对这些网站的隐私做法负责。

7. 儿童隐私

我们的应用面向一般受众，并非专为 13 岁以下儿童（或当地法律规定的更高年龄，如 16 岁）设计。我们不会在知情的情况下收集儿童的个人信息。如果你认为有儿童通过我们的应用提供了个人信息，请联系我们，我们会采取措施删除。

8. 数据保留与安全

保存在设备上的数据会一直保留，直到你删除或卸载应用。第三方服务收集的数据按其各自的政策保留。我们会采取合理措施保护信息，但任何存储或传输方式都无法做到绝对安全。

9. 你的权利

根据你所在地区的法律（例如 GDPR、英国 GDPR、CCPA/CPRA、中国《个人信息保护法》），你可能有权访问、更正或删除你的个人信息，反对或限制相关处理，并随时撤回同意。由于我们不在服务器上保存你的个人信息，大部分权利可以直接在设备上行使：在应用内删除数据、卸载应用、更改同意选择或重置广告标识符。对于第三方服务持有的数据，你可以直接联系他们，也可以联系我们，我们会尽力协助。你也有权向当地数据保护机构投诉。

10. 政策变更

我们可能会不时更新本隐私政策。更新内容将发布在本页面，并修改"最近更新"日期。请定期查看。

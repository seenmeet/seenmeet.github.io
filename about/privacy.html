DouZhuan Studio ("we", "us") cares about your privacy. This policy applies to all apps, games and mini programs / mini games we publish (together, the "Products"), whether you get them from the Apple App Store, Google Play, WeChat or another mini program platform, or any other channel. It explains what information the Products handle, why, who it is shared with, and the choices and rights you have.

Features differ between Products: anything described below as applying to "some Products" only applies to Products that offer that feature. If a Product handles data in a way this policy does not cover, we will say so on its store page or in the Product.

In short: our Products don't require an account and don't ask for your name, email or phone number; most game data stays on your device; and advertising, analytics, payments and mini program hosting are provided by the third-party services listed below. We do not sell your personal information.

1. Information we handle

1.1 Data that stays on your device

By default, game progress, saves, settings, records and scores, the unlock status of purchased items, and reminder times are stored only on your device and are not sent to us. They are deleted when you uninstall the app or delete the mini program.

1.2 Photos and files you choose

Some Products let you pick photos or files to create content. The Product can only read the items you select; they are processed and stored locally on your device and are never uploaded or shared with us or anyone else.

1.3 Network requests for content

When a Product downloads images, levels or other content from our content servers / CDN, those servers receive your IP address and basic technical data (such as the request time), as with any website. This is used only to deliver the content and keep the service running.

1.4 Mini programs and mini games

For mini programs running on WeChat or similar platforms, the platform gives us a pseudonymous identifier that distinguishes users (such as an OpenID). This does not give us your WeChat ID, nickname or phone number unless you explicitly agree to share them in the platform's permission prompt. Where a mini program offers cloud saves or leaderboards, that identifier and the related game data are stored in the cloud service provided by the platform, only to provide those features.

1.5 Information collected automatically by third parties

The advertising, analytics and payment services built into the Products (see Section 3) may automatically collect:

  • Device identifiers: on iOS, the Advertising Identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking) and the Identifier for Vendors (IDFV); on Android, the Advertising ID and App Set ID; and the Firebase app instance ID;
  • Device and app information such as device model, OS version, language, time zone and Product version;
  • Approximate region inferred from your IP address (we never read precise GPS location);
  • Usage data, such as which levels or content you play, the difficulty chosen, time taken, which features you use, and ad impressions and clicks;
  • Diagnostic data such as crash and performance information.

2. How we use information

  • To provide the Products' features, save your progress and deliver reminders you turn on (local notifications scheduled on your device, not sent through our servers);
  • To show ads, limit how often they appear, measure ad performance and prevent ad fraud;
  • To process and restore purchases;
  • To understand how features are used (in aggregate), fix problems and improve the Products;
  • To adjust Product settings remotely (for example, ad frequency or event content) without an update;
  • To keep the Products secure, prevent cheating and abuse, and comply with legal obligations.

5. Usage analytics

We use tools such as Firebase Analytics to understand aggregated usage (for example, which content is most popular and average completion times) so we can improve the Products. Analytics data is linked to a randomly generated identifier, does not include your name, photos or similar details, and we don't use it to identify you. In Products that offer a usage analytics switch, you can turn it off in Settings; once off, no analytics data is sent.

6. Purchases and payments

In-app payments are processed by Apple or Google, and payments in mini programs by WeChat Pay or the relevant platform. We never receive your card or account details; we only receive confirmation data such as the product ID, order and transaction status, to deliver, unlock and restore purchases.

7. Sharing and external links

Sharing results, images or other content uses your device's or platform's share function, and you decide who receives it. The Products may contain external links (for example, credits linking to an image's author); those websites' privacy practices are not covered by this policy.

8. Children and minors

Our Products are intended for a general audience and are not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Data on your device is kept until you delete it or uninstall the Product. When information is no longer needed, it is deleted or anonymized so that it cannot be recovered. We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Third-party services may process data outside your country or region (for example, in the United States), with safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses as described in their policies.
